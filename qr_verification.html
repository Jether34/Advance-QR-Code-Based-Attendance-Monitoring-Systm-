🔧 Troubleshooting

If QR codes are not scanning:

  • Size: Make sure QR code is at least 2x2 cm when printed
  • Quality: Ensure high contrast (black on white background)
  • Distance: Hold scanner 6-12 inches away from QR code
  • Lighting: Use good lighting, avoid glare and shadows
  • Angle: Hold scanner straight, not at an angle
  • Damage: Check for smudges, scratches, or printing issues
